# Official Cars — Dealer Partnership Agreement Outline

This is a business-planning outline, NOT a legal contract.

## 1. Parties
Official Cars and the participating dealership/business.

## 2. Program
Choose one or more:
- Authorized inventory listing
- Qualified lead generation
- Referral click
- Appointment lead
- Sponsored placement
- Other agreed marketing service

## 3. Compensation
Define the exact trigger and amount:
- Fixed fee per qualified lead
- Percentage of an agreed transaction value
- Fixed fee per completed sale
- Monthly listing/sponsorship fee
- Hybrid

State exactly when a commission is earned, excluded transactions, attribution window, cancellation rules and payment timing.

## 4. Inventory permissions
Dealer authorizes Official Cars to display agreed:
- vehicle data
- photos
- dealership name/logo
- address
- phone/contact information
- links

Dealer confirms it has rights to provide those materials.

## 5. Accuracy and updates
Dealer is responsible for supplying current pricing, availability and vehicle information. Official Cars should display an update timestamp and provide a process to remove sold vehicles.

## 6. Advertising disclosures
Paid/sponsored placements and financial relationships must be presented clearly. The parties should not create misleading impressions about independence, pricing, availability or endorsements.

## 7. Leads and privacy
Define what customer information is collected, consent language, where leads are sent, retention, security and each party's privacy obligations.

## 8. Compliance
Require both parties to comply with applicable federal, New York State and local advertising, consumer-protection, privacy and motor-vehicle rules.

## 9. Termination
Explain how either party can terminate, what happens to active listings and when unpaid commissions are settled.

## 10. Legal review
Have a New York attorney convert this outline into a contract before signing with dealerships.
